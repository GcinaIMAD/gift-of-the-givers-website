About Gift of the Givers

Gift of the Givers is a humanitarian organisation focused on providing assistance to people affected by disasters, poverty and humanitarian emergencies.

The organisation carries out humanitarian work in response to the needs of communities and individuals facing difficult circumstances.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide practical humanitarian assistance and support to people and communities in need.

Our Areas of Work

  • Humanitarian relief
  • Disaster response
  • Food and basic needs
  • Community support
  • Emergency assistance
