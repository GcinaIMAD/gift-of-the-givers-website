Welcome to Gift of the Givers

Gift of the Givers is a humanitarian organisation that provides assistance to people and communities affected by disasters, poverty and humanitarian crises.

The organisation responds to emergencies and provides support to communities in need through humanitarian and development projects.

Making a Difference

Gift of the Givers works to provide practical assistance to people facing difficult circumstances. Its work includes humanitarian relief, food assistance, disaster response and community support.

How You Can Help

  • Donate towards humanitarian projects.
  • Support communities affected by disasters.
  • Volunteer and contribute your skills.
  • Share information about humanitarian initiatives.
